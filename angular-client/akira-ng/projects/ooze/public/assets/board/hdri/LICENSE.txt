These are used only as image-based lighting: the renderer convolves each into
an irradiance map (PMREM) and never draws it as a backdrop. That is why 1k is
enough — a rough-surfaced dungeon shows none of the detail a larger one carries,
and the same file at 8k is 98 MB.
